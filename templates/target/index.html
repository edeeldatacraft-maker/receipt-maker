Free Target Receipt Template

Target-style receipts are known for their tidy, minimal register slip: short header, clean item rows, and a simple totals block. This generic sample captures that modern discount-retail look for small businesses that want receipts that feel current and uncluttered. It works well for boutiques, gift shops, and pop-up stalls where the receipt is part of the brand experience. Edit the store details, drop in your items, set your tax rate, and export a polished PDF in under a minute — no design software and no signup.

How to Create a Target Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom Target style receipt:

  1. Enter your store name and contact details; upload your logo for a branded header.
  2. List the items sold, adding quantities where customers buy multiples.
  3. Apply any discounts or promo codes on the dedicated discount line.
  4. Review the auto-computed subtotal, tax, and total, then select the payment method.
  5. Add your return-policy wording to the footer and download the finished receipt.
