Free Asian Restaurant Receipt Template

Asian restaurant receipts often mix course-based items, per-piece pricing, and service charges on one slip. This Asian-restaurant generic sample captures that format for sushi bars, noodle shops, and pan-Asian restaurants. It supports set menus and courses as grouped items, per-piece pricing for sushi, and the service-charge lines common across the cuisine. Use it for dine-in billing, takeaway orders, or staff training on course-based ticketing.

Template Specifications

Format Asian restaurant slip, course-grouped item layout
Fields Restaurant header, table number, course/item lines, quantities, service charge, subtotal, tax, total, payment method
Best for Sushi bars, noodle shops, pan-Asian restaurants, bubble-tea shops
Customization Restaurant name and logo, service-charge rate, tax rules, footer message

Who uses this template

Sushi bars itemizing per-piece orders, noodle shops with high table turnover, and Asian restaurants that need service-charge lines on every bill.

How to Create a Asian Restaurant Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom Asian Restaurant style receipt:

  1. Set your restaurant name, address, and service-charge rate.
  2. Add the table number, then list courses or items in serving order.
  3. Enter quantities for shared plates and per-piece sushi.
  4. Review the service charge, tax, and grand total.
  5. Present the bill at the table or download the PDF for records.

Asian Restaurant Receipt Template FAQs

Is this affiliated with any restaurant group?

No. It is a generic sample in an Asian-restaurant style, not affiliated with any brand, and not valid as proof of purchase.

Can I add a service charge?

Yes. A configurable service-charge line applies automatically to the subtotal.

Does it handle per-piece sushi pricing?

Yes. Items can be priced per piece with quantities for each selection.

Can I group courses together?

Yes. Items can be grouped under headings like appetizers, mains, and dessert.

Is it suitable for takeaway?

Yes. Takeaway orders use the same layout with a pickup-time note.
