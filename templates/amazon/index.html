Free Amazon Order Receipt Template

Online-order receipts look nothing like register slips: order numbers, shipment groups, and itemized packing detail. This Amazon-style generic sample recreates the e-commerce order summary for online sellers who want professional packing slips and order confirmations. It organizes items by shipment, shows order and tracking numbers, and itemizes shipping and tax per order — the details that cut "where is my order" messages. Ideal for marketplace sellers, Shopify stores, and anyone fulfilling orders from a home office.

Template Specifications

Format E-commerce order summary, shipment-grouped layout
Fields Order number, order date, shipment groups, item lines with quantities, shipping charges, tax, order total, payment method
Best for Marketplace sellers, Shopify/WooCommerce stores, home-office fulfillment
Customization Seller name and logo, accent color, order-number format, footer policies

Who uses this template

Etsy, eBay, and Amazon marketplace sellers who include packing slips, small Shopify stores without built-in slip designers, and dropshippers documenting orders for bookkeeping.

How to Create a Amazon Order Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom Amazon Order style receipt:

  1. Enter your seller name and upload your logo; set your accent color.
  2. Add the order number, date, and group items by shipment if the order ships in parts.
  3. Enter quantities, item prices, shipping charges, and tax.
  4. Review the order total and payment method line.
  5. Download as PDF to include in the package or PNG to message the buyer.
