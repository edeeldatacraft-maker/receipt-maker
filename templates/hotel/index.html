Hotel Template

Free Hotel Receipt Template

Hotel folios look nothing like register slips: guest name, check-in and check-out dates, nightly rates, and taxes — all on one itemized bill. This hotel receipt generator recreates that folio layout as a generic sample for hosts and small hotels that need professional guest bills without a full property-management system. Use it for guest checkouts, booking confirmations, expense documentation, or staff training on front-desk billing. Every field is editable — hotel name, dates, room charges, taxes — and the finished folio downloads as PDF or PNG.

Template Specifications

Format Hotel folio layout, single-page itemized guest bill
Fields Hotel header, guest name, check-in/check-out dates, room number, nightly rate, additional charges, taxes, total, payment method
Best for Small hotels, B&Bs, guesthouses, and vacation rental hosts
Customization Hotel name and logo, tax rate, folio footer message

Who uses this template

Independent hotels and bed-and-breakfasts issuing guest folios at checkout, Airbnb and vacation rental hosts documenting stays for guests, and hospitality students practicing front-desk billing.

How to Create a Hotel Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom hotel folio receipt:

  1. Enter your hotel name, address, and logo; set your local tax rate.
  2. Add the guest name, check-in and check-out dates, and room number.
  3. List the nightly rate and any extra charges (meals, minibar, parking).
  4. Review the auto-calculated taxes and folio total, then record the payment method.
  5. Download the PDF for the guest or print it at the front desk.

Hotel Receipt Template FAQs

Is my guest data saved?

No. Folios are generated in your browser; nothing is uploaded unless you create an account and choose to save.

Can I print this on a real thermal printer?

Yes. The layout follows the standard 80mm thermal roll width, so it prints cleanly on most receipt printers.

Does it calculate tax automatically?

Yes. Enter your local tax rate once and every item total, subtotal, and grand total updates instantly.

Can I add a barcode?

Yes. A barcode footer is generated from the transaction number and prints at the bottom of the receipt.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. Guest sessions run entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded unless you create an account and choose to save.
