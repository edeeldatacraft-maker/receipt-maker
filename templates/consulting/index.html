Free Consulting Receipt Template

Consulting receipts are really fee summaries: who you advised, for how long, and at what rate. This consulting generic sample gives coaches, advisors, and agencies a clean, credible receipt for client billing. It handles hourly billing, day rates, and retainers, with space for project references. Use it alongside invoices, for expense claims, or as standalone proof of payment for advisory work.

Template Specifications

Format Professional fee summary, minimal corporate layout
Fields Consultant header, client name, engagement reference, billing lines (hours/days/retainer), rate, subtotal, tax, total, payment method
Best for Business consultants, coaches, marketing agencies, advisors
Customization Logo, billing units, tax settings, payment terms note

Who uses this template

Independent consultants billing hourly, business coaches receipting package payments, and agencies documenting advisory fees.

How to Create a Consulting Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom Consulting style receipt:

  1. Add your business name, logo, and contact details.
  2. Enter the client name and engagement or project reference.
  3. Add billing lines — hours, days, or a fixed retainer.
  4. Review the rate math, tax, and total.
  5. Download the PDF to attach to your invoice or send standalone.

Consulting Receipt Template FAQs

Is this an invoice?

No — it is a receipt (proof of payment). Many consultants attach it to their invoice once paid.

Can I bill hourly and per-project?

Yes. Lines support hourly, daily, or fixed-fee billing.

Does it handle retainers?

Yes. Retainer periods bill as single lines with date ranges.

Can I add payment terms?

Yes. A terms note field covers payment method and timing.

Is it suitable for agencies?

Yes. Agencies use it for client-facing proof of payment on advisory work.
