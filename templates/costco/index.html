Free Costco Wholesale Receipt Template

Warehouse-club receipts have a distinctive dense format: membership number, long item lists with item codes, and instant-savings lines. This Costco-style generic sample reproduces that bulk-retail layout for businesses that sell in volume — wholesalers, cash-and-carry outlets, and buying clubs. It handles long item lists gracefully, supports member or account numbers on the header, and itemizes savings so customers see exactly what bulk buying saved them. As with all samples on this site, it is a generic mockup for bookkeeping and internal use, not an official document of any warehouse club.

Template Specifications

Format Dense warehouse-style slip, optimized for long item lists
Fields Member/account number, item codes, quantities, unit prices, instant-savings lines, subtotal, tax, total, payment method
Best for Wholesalers, cash-and-carry stores, buying clubs, bulk resellers
Customization Business name, membership-field label, tax rate, savings-line wording

Who uses this template

Wholesale distributors issuing itemized slips to resellers, buying clubs tracking member purchases, and bulk retailers who need item codes and quantities on every line for inventory reconciliation.

How to Create a Costco Wholesale Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom Costco Wholesale style receipt:

  1. Set your business name and label the member field (member ID, account number, or loyalty number).
  2. Add items with their codes and quantities; bulk pricing is handled per line.
  3. Enter any instant savings or volume discounts so they itemize separately.
  4. Verify the subtotal, tax, and total across the full item list.
  5. Download the PDF for your records or share a PNG with the customer.

Costco Receipt Template FAQs

Is this a real Costco receipt?

No. It is an independent generic sample in a warehouse-club style, not affiliated with or endorsed by Costco, and not valid as proof of purchase.

Can it handle very long item lists?

Yes. The layout is designed for bulk transactions and paginates cleanly on PDF export.

What is the member number field for?

It is an editable label — use it for member IDs, account numbers, or loyalty numbers in your own business.

Does it show savings per item?

Yes. Instant-savings lines itemize discounts so totals stay transparent.

Can I reuse it for repeat customers?

With a free account you can save business details and item lists to regenerate receipts faster.
