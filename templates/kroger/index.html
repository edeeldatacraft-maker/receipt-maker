Grocery Template

Free Kroger Grocery Receipt Template

Grocery receipts are their own format: dozens of short item lines, weighted produce entries, coupons, and loyalty savings. This Kroger-style generic sample mirrors the supermarket slip for independent grocers, butchers, bakeries, and farm shops. It supports per-weight pricing for produce, coupon lines that deduct cleanly, and a savings summary so shoppers see their total discounts. If you run a food retail business and still hand-write receipts, this template moves you to printed, itemized slips in minutes.

Template Specifications

Format Supermarket slip, high-density short item lines
Fields Store header, item lines with weight/qty support, coupon lines, loyalty-savings summary, subtotal, tax (on taxable items), total, tender and change
Best for Independent grocers, butchers, bakeries, farm shops, delis
Customization Store name and logo, tax rules, coupon-line labels, footer message

Who uses this template

Neighborhood grocery stores, butcher shops pricing by weight, bakeries with daily specials, and farmers-market vendors who want a professional slip without a full POS system.

How to Create a Kroger Grocery Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom Kroger Grocery style receipt:

  1. Enter your store name, address, and logo in the header.
  2. Add items line by line, using weight or quantity pricing for produce and deli goods.
  3. Apply coupons on the dedicated coupon lines so discounts stay visible.
  4. Check the savings summary, tax, and final total.
  5. Print or download — the slip format suits counter printers and PDF records alike.

Kroger Receipt Template FAQs

Is this affiliated with Kroger?

No. It is a generic supermarket-style sample, not affiliated with or endorsed by Kroger.

Does it handle weighted items?

Yes. Produce and deli lines support price-per-weight entry.

Can I show coupon savings separately?

Yes. Coupon lines deduct individually and roll into a savings summary.

Is tax calculated on the right items?

You set your local tax rules; the template applies tax only where you configure it.

Can I use it at a market stall?

Yes. It is designed to work from any browser, so a tablet at your stall is enough.
