Free Home Cleaning Receipt Template

Cleaning receipts need to show exactly what was cleaned, for how long, and what it cost. This home-cleaning generic sample is built for cleaners, maid services, and property managers who need professional job receipts without invoicing software. It itemizes rooms or tasks, hours worked, supplies used, and recurring-service discounts. Use it after every clean — one-off or scheduled — for client records and your own bookkeeping.

How to Create a Home Cleaning Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom Home Cleaning style receipt:

  1. Enter your business name, contact details, and logo.
  2. Add the client name, service date, and property address.
  3. List the rooms cleaned or tasks completed, with hours per line.
  4. Add supplies charges or a recurring-service discount if applicable.
  5. Download the PDF for the client or print it on the spot.

Cleaning Receipt Template FAQs

Is this a tax invoice?

It is a generic service receipt sample for record-keeping. For tax invoices, check your local requirements before relying on it.

Can I bill by the hour?

Yes. Task lines support hourly rates with hours worked, or flat per-job pricing.

Does it handle recurring cleans?

Yes. A recurring-service discount line keeps weekly or fortnightly pricing clear.

Can I list cleaning supplies separately?

Yes. Supplies can be itemized on their own lines or bundled into the service total.

Is client data stored?

No. Receipts are generated in your browser; nothing is uploaded unless you create an account.
