Pharmacy Template

Free Pharmacy Receipt Template

Pharmacy receipts list medications, quantities, and co-pays in a compact, scannable format. This pharmacy generic sample mirrors that dispensary slip for independent pharmacies and clinics. It itemizes prescriptions and over-the-counter products separately, shows co-pay and insurance portions, and keeps the layout familiar to patients. Use it at the counter, for delivery orders, or as a refill record.

Template Specifications

Format Dispensary slip, compact itemized layout
Fields Pharmacy header, patient name, prescription/OTC lines, quantities, co-pay, insurance portion, subtotal, tax, total, payment method
Best for Independent pharmacies, clinic dispensaries, online pharmacies
Customization Pharmacy logo, pharmacist line, insurance fields

Who uses this template

Independent pharmacies receipting prescriptions, clinic dispensaries documenting dispensed medicines, and online pharmacies confirming orders.

How to Create a Pharmacy Receipt Online

Follow these simple steps to make your own custom Pharmacy style receipt:

  1. Add your pharmacy name, address, and pharmacist details.
  2. Enter the patient name and fill date.
  3. List prescriptions and over-the-counter items with quantities.
  4. Split co-pay and insurance portions where applicable.
  5. Download the PDF or print it at the counter.

Pharmacy Receipt Template FAQs

Is this a valid prescription record?

It is a generic receipt sample for record-keeping, not a medical or legal prescription document.

Can I separate prescription and OTC items?

Yes. Prescription and over-the-counter items list in separate sections.

Does it show co-pays?

Yes. Co-pay and insurance-covered portions can be split on the receipt.

Can I add a pharmacist line?

Yes. An editable pharmacist/dispenser line prints on every receipt.

Is patient data stored?

No. Everything is generated in your browser.
