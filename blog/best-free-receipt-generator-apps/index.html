In-Depth App Reviews

Key highlights include:

  • Custom Branding: Easily upload your business logo, customize fonts, choose brand colors, and add custom footer policy notes.

  • Completely Free & Clean: No watermarks, no hidden upgrade screens, and unlimited receipt generation.

The interface is fully optimized for mobile devices, allowing you to generate and deliver receipts on the go in under 60 seconds.

2. Invoice Simple (Invoice-Generator.com)

Invoice Simple is a well-established billing tool that offers a basic free invoice and receipt generator. It is clean and features an intuitive fill-in-the-blanks layout.

However, it has a few drawbacks for general receipt generation:

  • It only exports to PDF format. If you need a quick image file (PNG/JPG) to text to a client, you will have to manually convert it.
  • The free version places a promotional watermark in the footer, which can look less professional for client-facing transactions.
  • It lacks specialized thermal paper templates or retailer-specific styles.

3. ExpressExpense

ExpressExpense is a popular tool for users who specifically need physical thermal receipt layouts (like taxi slips, restaurant itemizations, or fuel receipts).

While their templates look highly realistic, the app is not truly free:

  • You must create an account and sign up to use the tool.
  • The free version is extremely limited and places a large, obtrusive watermark across the generated document.
  • Unlocking clean PDF exports requires a monthly subscription, making it less ideal for occasional users.

4. Custom Receipt Maker

Custom Receipt Maker is one of the oldest receipt-building tools on the web. It uses a very basic HTML form that outputs a legacy text-based receipt layout.

While it is completely free and requires no registration, it has significant limitations in 2026:

  • The user interface is dated and not mobile-friendly.
  • There is no direct PDF or image download button; you must rely on your browser's "Print Page to PDF" feature.
  • The template styles look like dated 90s terminal outputs, lacking the premium, modern aesthetic customers expect today.

5. FreeInvoiceCreator

FreeInvoiceCreator is a straightforward tool that helps users generate basic invoices and payment receipts. It features a modern, clean interface and calculates taxes automatically.

However, it has a notable usage barrier:

  • It requires users to sign up and submit an email address before they are allowed to download their finished PDF receipt.
  • It only supports standard A4 invoice layouts and does not offer specialized receipt templates (like thermal, retail, or service receipts).
